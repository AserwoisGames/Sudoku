About the app

Sudoku – learn & play is a classic Sudoku game for Android. Most Sudoku games just hand you puzzles – this one shows you how to solve them.

  • 16 solving techniques explained step by step, with practice puzzles
  • Hints that explain which technique to use and why
  • XP, levels and badges for every puzzle and lesson
  • Daily challenge, weekly challenge and a player leaderboard
  • 4 difficulty levels, light and dark theme, no ads

Signing in with Google

You can play without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and is used only to keep your progress when you change phones, to show you on the leaderboard under a nickname and to let you play the weekly challenge. The app receives your email address and basic Google profile details; it never receives your password.
